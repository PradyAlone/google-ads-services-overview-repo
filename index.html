Service types

Each service plays a different role in local visibility. Use the cards below to explain where the ad appears, what the client pays for, and why it matters.

PPC Search Ads

PPC
Pay-per-click

Text-based search ads that appear when someone searches keywords related to a service, location, or buying need.

Best fit: targeted search demand and measurable Landing page + Call traffic.
Google Business Profile + Map Packs

GBP
Map Pack

Local business visibility connected to the Google Business Profile, including maps, reviews, location relevance, and local discovery.

Best fit: local presence, reviews, calls, and map visibility.
Local Service Ads

LSA
Local Services

Lead-focused ads for eligible local service categories, commonly shown with trust signals like screening status, reviews, and direct contact options.

Best fit: high-intent local leads and direct customer contact.
